<h1 align="center">macfreeapps</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Useful macOS apps, built in the open.</strong></p>

Our goal is to open-source the small, useful apps people need on macOS. We build focused tools, share their source, and welcome contributions from the community.

### Apps

- [**komPX**](https://github.com/macfreeapps/kompx) — Compress images and videos locally on your Mac.
- [**FanPilot**](https://github.com/macfreeapps/fanpilot) — Check your Mac's temperature and fan speed, and manage fan behavior.
